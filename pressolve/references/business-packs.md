# Business Website Packs

Use these as optional domain checklists. Verify the specific products and legal/business rules before implementation.

## Membership and community

Cover registration, verification, roles/capabilities, protected content, subscriptions, renewals, cancellation, failed payments, account deletion/export, email, moderation, and data portability. Test role escalation, expired access, duplicate webhooks, refunds, and cache exclusions.

## LMS and courses

Cover course structure, prerequisites, enrollment, progress, quizzes, certificates, instructor roles, media delivery, accessibility, reporting, payments, and migration/export. Verify progress and completion after updates.

## Booking and appointments

Cover services/resources, staff, availability, timezone/DST, buffers, capacity, deposits/payments, cancellation, reminders, calendar sync, double-booking prevention, and privacy. Test simultaneous booking and provider outage.

## Subscriptions

Cover recurring billing, trials, upgrades/downgrades, proration, renewals, retries, dunning, cancellation, refunds, tax, entitlements, webhooks, and reconciliation. Never test with live charges unless explicitly authorized.

## Directories and marketplaces

Cover listing ownership, moderation, search/filter/map, claims, submissions, media, reviews, messaging, payments/commissions, disputes, abuse prevention, privacy, SEO, and data export.

## Multilingual and regional sites

Cover language routing, translations, menus/templates, localized URLs, hreflang, canonical rules, currencies/tax, forms/email, search, media, and fallback content. Avoid mixing multiple multilingual systems.

## Nonprofit sites

Cover donation gateways, recurring donations, receipts, donor privacy, campaign tracking, accessibility, volunteer/event workflows, email integration, and reconciliation.

## Agency and client maintenance

Cover environment/access inventory, approved change windows, backups, update policy, licenses, monitoring, incident contacts, reports, handover, ownership, and offboarding. Never centralize client secrets in reports or shared chat.

## Real estate

Use [real-estate.md](real-estate.md) for IDX, MLS, RESO, listing compliance, property search, CRM, maps, and lead routing.
